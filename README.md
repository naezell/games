# Games

Small games built just for fun.

## Frostfall Camp (`frostfall-camp/`)

A snowy camp-builder: hunt frost boars, cook meat on the grill, sell steaks to villagers,
then spend the cash on build pads (sawmill, workers, watchtower, upgrades).
Build a bridge over the river to mine crystals for the Gem Cutter, and defend your food
from wolves that raid the camp at night (build Camp Gates to slow them down).
Fish off the dock for the Smokehouse, and every third day take on the Ice Bear.
Finish daily goals for bonus cash, run a Hot Springs that villagers pay to soak in
(it burns logs), and adopt a husky that fetches loot and chases wolves.
Watch for snowstorms (steaks sell for double), buy boosts from the traveling trader,
hire a banker to collect cash, and unlock outfits at the wardrobe.
Ship goods by reindeer sled for better prices, climb the camp ranks (fireworks and
bonus cash), and use the minimap in the corner to find wolves, the bear and the trader.

Across the river, found **Ridge Camp**: a Lantern Shop (logs + crystals = lanterns), a
Market Stall, a Yak Pen whose yaks grow wool, and a Tailor that sells coats, with
crafter, shepherd and runner workers. The **Town Hall** sells 17 multi-level upgrades
and 7 decorations, including a Golden Statue (+10% income) and an Aurora Beacon
(passive income).

From Ridge Camp, open the **Frost Mine**: a dark cavern with gold veins, a Goldsmith,
roaming ice golems and the **Crystal King** boss. **Trophies** (28 of them) track your
lifetime feats, and the Town Hall's **Legacy** tab offers a Golden Restart: start over
for Golden Stars that permanently boost income.

Take the **Aurora Ferry** to Aurora Isle (penguins, aurora shards, a Shard Exchange
and an Ice Hotel that uses lanterns). Buy a **fishing boat**, adopt a **snow owl** that
attacks enemies anywhere, hire **Gold Diggers** in the mine, follow the weekly **boss
calendar** (Ice Bear, Wolf Alpha, Yeti), and hire a **Camp Manager** who earns money
while you're away.

**Crafting chains:** berries from the west meadow become jam at the Jam Kitchen; coal
and iron ore from the Frost Mine become iron bars at the Smelter, then tools at the
Blacksmith; the Ice Saw cuts river ice for the Ice Carver; snowblooms and aurora shards
become potions at the Apothecary. **Market Town**, south of camp, has the General Store
that buys jam, tools and potions. New workers (berry picker, store clerk, smelter hand,
herbalist) and Town Hall haulers (Mine Cart, Ice Wagon, Potion Courier) automate it.
Also in Market Town: a Bee Yard and Candle Maker, a Furniture Shop (logs into chairs) and
a Jeweler (crystal + gold = rings); south of camp, a Pie Bakery (meat + berries). South of Ridge Camp: a Milking Barn (yaks give
milk), a Creamery (milk into cheese) and a Toy Workshop (wool + log = toy bear). By the
river, a Glassblower turns ice and coal into ornaments.

**Land plots:** misty land rings the map. Once the sawmill is built, pads at the map's edge
sell 14 named plots (east-side plots need the bridge; corner plots open from the plot beside
them). Buying one lifts the mist, grows a forest and brings three boars into its clearing.
Each plot costs more than the last ($2,000, then about 35% more each time). Bought land stays
yours through a Golden Restart; the Restart button clears it.

- **Play:** open `frostfall-camp/index.html` in any browser (needs internet once to load three.js).
- **Controls:** drag anywhere on the screen (phone) or WASD / arrow keys (computer).
- Chopping and attacking happen automatically when you walk next to a tree or boar.
- Progress saves in your browser; the Restart button wipes it.

**Latest additions:** a Menu with fast travel (also by tapping the minimap), a live Warehouse,
a backup code to save or move your progress, a Check for update button, photo mode and
optional music. Villager orders at the Order Board, a Farm (wheat and carrots into bread and
stew), spring thaws every fourth day, the Ember Volcano (through a lava tunnel in the Frost
Mine) with obsidian, fire golems and a Lava Forge for obsidian blades, Winter Festivals
(+25% income) and siege nights, a riding reindeer, and Penguin Pal and Treasure Fox pets.

**Even more:** building tiers (Silver, Gold) and golden pieces worth 5x, conveyor belts, a
Tinker Bench with hidden recipes, an Armory for weapons and armor, the Ice Cave dungeon with
the Frost Wraith, bosses that level up, villagers who move in, a six-chapter story, weekly
contests against a rival camp, Sky Peak, the Frozen Lake, the Steam Caves and Shipwreck Cove,
an animal sanctuary, pet treats, a 20-fish collection book, garden pieces you can place and
move, sunny spells, blizzards and aurora nights, and four holidays.

### Round F: automation, challenges, people, extras
- **Freight Train** loops north of camp and sells goods from any building more than half full. **Storage Sheds** let every building hold more. The **Power Plant** burns logs to speed up all buildings. A new **Diamond** tier adds a second floor. **Camp beauty**: every 10 garden pieces gives +1% income, and there are four new garden pieces.
- **Crossbow turrets** shoot wolves. The Ice Cave now goes down into the **Endless Depths**. A **Frost Titan** appears each week, and damage to it lasts all week. **Timed challenges** award medals. **Hard rules** for Golden Restarts give extra stars.
- **Villager favors** at 4 and 8 hearts, each adding +1% income. A **merchant ship** docks every third day. **Duels** against Captain Brisk. **Lantern Parade** and the **Carving Cup**. A **mailbox** with tips, gifts and paid requests.
- New pets: the **Snow Hare** and the **Baby Yeti**. A **Cooking Pot** with 8 recipes that give buffs. An **Aquarium** that earns more for every fish species you've caught. A **Snowmobile**. **Ambient sounds** (wind, water, birds, fire) and festival music.

### Round G: harbor, managers, dens, council
- The **Cargo Balloon** brings goods home from Ridge Camp and the far lands. **Area managers** (in the Town Hall) collect cash piles for you. The **Water Mill** powers buildings for free. The **Harbor District** has a Fish Market, Shipyard, Café and Gift Shop, and research unlocks the Boot Maker, Sled Works and Ski Shop there.
- **Wolf dens** to clear each week, **avalanches** that bury buildings, the **Deep Hole** ice-fishing mini-game, a weekly **Lake Tournament** against the villagers, and **daily streak** rewards.
- **Villager jobs**. **Six new villagers** with homes facing Market Town. A **tourist ferry** that brings shoppers to the Gift Shop. **Story chapters 7–10**, ending with the harbor beacon. A weekly **Town Council** vote.
