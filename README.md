# Games

Small games built just for fun.

## Frostfall Camp (`frostfall-camp/`)

A snowy camp-builder: hunt frost boars, cook meat on the grill, sell steaks to villagers,
then spend the cash on build pads (sawmill, workers, watchtower, upgrades).
Build a bridge over the river to mine crystals for the Gem Cutter, and defend your food
from wolves that raid the camp at night (build Camp Gates to slow them down).
Fish off the dock for the Smokehouse, and every third day take on the Ice Bear.
Finish daily goals for bonus cash, run a Hot Springs that villagers pay to soak in
(it burns logs), and adopt a husky that fetches loot and chases wolves.
Watch for snowstorms (steaks sell for double), buy boosts from the traveling trader,
hire a banker to collect cash, and unlock outfits at the wardrobe.
Ship goods by reindeer sled for better prices, climb the camp ranks (fireworks and
bonus cash), and use the minimap in the corner to find wolves, the bear and the trader.

Across the river, found **Ridge Camp**: a Lantern Shop (logs + crystals = lanterns), a
Market Stall, a Yak Pen whose yaks grow wool, and a Tailor that sells coats, with
crafter, shepherd and runner workers. The **Town Hall** sells 17 multi-level upgrades
and 7 decorations, including a Golden Statue (+10% income) and an Aurora Beacon
(passive income).

From Ridge Camp, open the **Frost Mine**: a dark cavern with gold veins, a Goldsmith,
roaming ice golems and the **Crystal King** boss. **Trophies** (28 of them) track your
lifetime feats, and the Town Hall's **Legacy** tab offers a Golden Restart: start over
for Golden Stars that permanently boost income.

Take the **Aurora Ferry** to Aurora Isle (penguins, aurora shards, a Shard Exchange
and an Ice Hotel that uses lanterns). Buy a **fishing boat**, adopt a **snow owl** that
attacks enemies anywhere, hire **Gold Diggers** in the mine, follow the weekly **boss
calendar** (Ice Bear, Wolf Alpha, Yeti), and hire a **Camp Manager** who earns money
while you're away.

- **Play:** open `frostfall-camp/index.html` in any browser (needs internet once to load three.js).
- **Controls:** drag anywhere on the screen (phone) or WASD / arrow keys (computer).
- Chopping and attacking happen automatically when you walk next to a tree or boar.
- Progress saves in your browser; the Restart button wipes it.
