How hauling works

Carts take two seconds per tile each way, plus four seconds to load and unload. Trips cost $1 per leg of distance, charged as a round trip. Building closer saves both time and cash. Carts wait for a full load. When a cart supplies the furniture shop, the mill reserves its next six planks if the shop is low on stock.
